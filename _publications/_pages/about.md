# About Me

**Junteng Liu** is a first-year PhD candidate at the **HKUST NLP Group**, Hong Kong University of Science and Technology, advised by Professor Junxian He. He received his B.Eng. (Computer Science) from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU), graduating in June 2024.

## Academic Background

- **Ph.D. in Computer Science (2024–Present)** — Hong Kong University of Science and Technology
- **B.Eng. (2020–2024)** — Shanghai Jiao Tong University
- **Award:** Zhiyuan Honor Scholarship (Shanghai Jiao Tong University)

## Research Experience

- **Research Intern**, MINIMAX (Feb 2025 – Present)
- **Research Intern**, Tencent WXG (Jun 2024 – Sep 2024), advised by Zifei Shan
- **Research Intern**, Shanghai AI Lab (Jun 2023 – Dec 2023), advised by Prof. Yu Cheng

## Research Interests

Natural language processing and machine learning, with a focus on:

- LLM reasoning and reinforcement learning
- Hallucination in vision-language models (VLMs)
- LLM truthfulness and interpretability
